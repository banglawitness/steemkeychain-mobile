Privacy Policy

Steem Keychain · Last updated 11 November 2026

Steem Keychain is a non-custodial wallet. Your private keys stay on your phone. This app does not run an account server, and it does not receive your keys, PIN, or fingerprint.

What stays on your phone

  • Account names and encrypted keys, locked with your PIN.
  • Optional fingerprint or Face ID unlock.
  • Browser history, bookmarks, and saved recipient names.
  • The Steem node you choose, theme, and notification settings.

What leaves your phone

  • Steem requests go to the node you select. That node sees the account name and the signed transaction. It does not receive your private key.
  • STEEM and SBD prices are loaded from CoinGecko. That request does not include your keys or PIN.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Photos are not uploaded.

Sharing

The app does not sell your data and does not show ads.

Delete your data

Open Settings and use Erase. This removes the wallet from this phone. Uninstalling the app also removes the local data.
